<div class="play">

 

  <p class="stage">(A Sunday. Glorious weather. The New Student and the First Student in a small rowing boat on the lake. The First Student holds a fishing rod.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">I can feel something.</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(They both look at the line. It begins to move.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">I think it's a little one.</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The line goes slack and still.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Let's wait for a bigger one.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I feel like I'm on the hook.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Because of that surprise visit the other day?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">Yes. It was such an embarrassment. I can see it in the others' eyes.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Papperlapp.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">Easy for you to say.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">I only speak the truth.</p>

 

  <p class="beat">(A beat.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Always.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">Don't tell me you never lie.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Not in the common sense.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">She told me she had only accepted because she wanted to look after me. To see if I'm happy here.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Are you?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I'm not sure. Right now, on this lake... with you... I am.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Good to hear.</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The line begins to move violently.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Oh, that's a big one!</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The line pulls hard. The rod bends. The boat rocks. There is a struggle for a minute.)</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The line goes slack.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Gone.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I thought this would be easier.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">It's always a struggle.</p>

 

  <p class="beat">(A beat.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Everything is.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">You make it look so easy.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">The fishing?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">It's as if... you belong here.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Me? The scholarship boy?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">Yes. You.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">I'm taking in as much as I can.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I hope I'm going to survive next week.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">You will.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">You are always so... so confident.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">When it comes to you and your mum, I am.</p>

 

  <p class="beat">(A beat.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">Didn't you find her small... next to Catherine?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Not at all. She's the future.</p>

 

  <p class="beat">(A beat.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">And you will get your horse. I'd say there will be a trailer coming next week.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">For a new movie?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">With your horse.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I very much doubt that.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">You wanna bet?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">A tenner?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">It would be unethical of me to accept this.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I don't see why.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Do I really have to spell it out?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I'm quite lost. Why don't you?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">I talked to a certain someone.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">To him?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Yes. To him.</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">And he will buy me a horse?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Don't be so thick.</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The line begins to move. Not quite as violently as before, but the rod bends. A brief struggle ensues. The First Student brings out a fish, some thirty centimetres long.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">A perch!</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The First Student takes the fish in his hands and holds it up.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Look at these colours.</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(He unhooks it carefully.)</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The First Student</span>

  <p class="line">Shall we give him back his freedom?</p>

 

  <span class="speaker">The New Student</span>

  <p class="line">I think we should eat him.</p>

 

  <p class="stage">(The First Student kills the fish with a sharp blow to the head.)</p>
